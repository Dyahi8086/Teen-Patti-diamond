Privacy Policy

Teen Patti Diamond · Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains the limited information that may be processed when you use Teen Patti Diamond. The app uses Supabase authentication and may use Google or Facebook sign-in, or anonymous guest sign-in, depending on the option you choose.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Sign-in information: when you use Google or Facebook, the authentication provider may share basic account information needed to sign you in, such as an account identifier, name, email address, or profile image, subject to that provider’s settings.
  • Guest sign-in: guest play uses an anonymous authentication session. It may create an internal user identifier.
  • Profile information: the app's Supabase database may store a display name, avatar URL, and account timestamps for your player profile.
  • Technical information: the hosting platform, browser, or authentication service may process technical data needed to deliver, secure, and operate the app.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate players, maintain sign-in sessions, display player profiles, support app features, protect the service, and troubleshoot errors.

3. Payments and real-money play

The app is designed for entertainment only. It does not provide real-money betting, cash prizes, deposits, or withdrawals.

4. Service providers

The app uses Supabase for authentication and profile data. Google or Facebook may process information when you choose their sign-in option. GitHub Pages or its delivery infrastructure may serve the app files. Each provider handles information under its own privacy terms.

5. Storage and security

Authentication sessions and profile information may be stored by the relevant browser and service providers. Reasonable platform security features are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the app and sign out. You can also review or manage permissions with the sign-in provider you used. Deleting an app session from your device may not automatically delete records held by authentication or database providers.

7. Children and family use

If you are under the age required to consent to online services in your location, use the app only with permission and supervision from a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as app features or service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the app owner or project maintainer through the contact method provided with the project.
